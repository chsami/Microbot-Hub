# Fourtifys Gem Mining

Mines gem rocks in the Shilo Village gem mine. When the inventory is
full, it walks to the bank, banks the uncut gems and returns to mine.
It also fills and empties a gem bag if you carry one.

Members only: Shilo Village is a members area, so play on a members
world.

| Mine | Mining level | Requirements | Gem rocks | Banking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Surface | 40 | Shilo Village quest | 7 | Shilo Village bank |
| Underground | 40 | Shilo Village quest and the medium Karamja Diary | 48 | Bank deposit chest in the mine |

## Setup

1. Log in to a members world and meet the requirements of your mine in
   the table above.
2. Wear or carry a pickaxe you have the Mining level to use.
3. Choose the mine in **Mine**.
4. Optional: carry a gem bag. If you use the bag yourself while the
   plugin runs, it waits a few ticks and checks the bag again.
5. Start near the mine and enable the plugin. It waits while scripts
   are paused from the client header pause button or by the Break
   Handler, and while you use the mouse or keyboard yourself.
