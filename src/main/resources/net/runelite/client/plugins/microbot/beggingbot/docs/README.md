# Begging Bot

Walks to the Grand Exchange, stands near other players and asks for gp in public chat every 5-10 seconds.

- Accepts incoming trades and presses Accept on both trade screens. It never adds an item or coins to its own side, and it declines a final confirmation that would have it give anything away.
- Hops to a populated world after 15 minutes without a trade, or when no player has been nearby for 20 seconds.
- On-screen paint shows time run, gold received, gold per hour, trades, world, players nearby and hops.

Settings: message interval, no-trade hop time, player radius, no-players hop time, and extra messages (separated by `|`).

Note: begging breaks the game rules; use at your own risk.
