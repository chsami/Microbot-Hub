# Inferno Prayers

Inferno assist by droplugins. It has two modes and no full-automatic mode.

## Modes

- **Prayers only** (default): the script switches protection and offensive prayers. You play everything else:
  movement, attacks, gear, potions and inventory. Prayers are sent as direct actions, without opening the
  prayer book or moving the mouse.
- **Auto no movement**: the script plays everything except walking: prayers, attacks, gear, potions, specials and Zuk stay automatic, and you move the player yourself. It only attacks targets in range and sight of your tile.

Changing the mode applies on the next run.

## Starting

Gear up yourself, stand beside the Inferno entrance and enable the plugin. The plugin does not load gear,
buy supplies or travel for you.

## Settings

- Mode
- Eat at HP %
- Ranging potions from wave
- Range only
- Zuk healers at once
- Show Inferno tile pack
- Log out after death or cape

The plugin's settings panel lists the suggested stats, gear and inventory.

## Requirements

Microbot client 2.6.55 or newer.
