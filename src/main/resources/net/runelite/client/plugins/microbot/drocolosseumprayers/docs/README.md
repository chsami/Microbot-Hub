# Colosseum Prayers

Fortis Colosseum assist by droplugins. It has two modes and no full-automatic mode.

## Modes

- **Prayers only** (default): the script switches protection and offensive prayers. You play everything else:
  movement, attacks, gear, potions and inventory. Prayers are sent as direct actions, without opening the
  prayer book or moving the mouse.
- **Auto no movement**: the script plays prayers, gear switches, potions, specials and wave starts; you walk and attack. It only attacks the Fremennik warband and casts the last-enemy Blood heal from where you stand. The Javelin sidestep stays automatic.

Changing the mode applies on the next run.

## Starting

Gear up yourself, stand beside the Fortis Colosseum entrance and enable the plugin. The plugin does not load gear,
buy supplies or travel for you.

## Settings

- Mode
- Eat at HP %
- Kill with
- Warband first
- Blood spells on the last enemy
- Potions from wave
- Show Colosseum tile pack
- Log out after death or completion

The plugin's settings panel lists the suggested stats, gear and inventory.

## Requirements

Microbot client 2.6.55 or newer.
