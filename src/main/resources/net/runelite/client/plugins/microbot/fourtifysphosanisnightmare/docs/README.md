# Fourtifys Phosani's Nightmare

Kills Phosani's Nightmare. It prays against her attacks, including
through her curse, steps off her rifts, spores, flowers and surges,
kills her husks, parasites and her blasts' sleepwalkers and charges the
totems. In her final phase it puts every attack on her, special attacks
first. Between kills it picks up the loot and disturbs her again. When
supplies run low, it teleports to Ferox Enclave with a Ring of dueling,
banks, restocks, drinks from the Pool of Refreshment and returns with
Drakan's medallion. It also teleports out mid-fight when food and Prayer
are both gone.

## Setup

1. Unlock the Slepe teleport on Drakan's medallion with a Slepey tablet.
2. Wear or carry a charged Ring of dueling, and keep spares and Drakan's
   medallion in your bank.
3. Put food, super restores or prayer potions, parasite cures and combat
   potions in your bank. Set bank withdrawals to **Item**. If your bank
   has a PIN, save it in your Microbot profile, or type it yourself when
   the plugin asks.
4. Enter your gear as comma-separated item IDs in **Melee gear IDs** and
   **Magic gear IDs**, and one item ID for each swap weapon. The defaults
   are a strong setup; replace them with your own.
5. Set the supplies, their thresholds and the **Next kill** minimums.
6. Optional: on the Arceuus spellbook, turn on **Ghostly thrall** for
   the totems and **Death Charge** before special attacks. The thrall
   needs the Book of the Dead and 2 to 6 Prayer points. Add the book
   and your rune pouch to **Keep items** (`25818,12791`) so banking
   leaves them in your inventory.
7. Enable the plugin at Ferox Enclave, in the Sisterhood Sanctuary or in
   her dream.

If you die, the plugin stops. Collect your items from Sister Senga
before restarting.

When the plugin stops, it says why and what to do next in the game chat
and with a RuneLite notification. It waits while Microbot scripts are
paused, including Break Handler breaks.

## Not supported

Collecting items after a death, the regular Nightmare, group fights and
ranged setups.
